What these counts mean

Views, starts and completed comparisons count once per page load. A completed comparison means both offers are valid after an edit. PDF downloads count after a report response is received and the browser starts the download; saves count after a successful save response.

Anonymous sessions use a random cookie that expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. Sessions are browser visits, not verified people. Daily session counts can overlap across days. Staff testing, browser privacy opt-outs and blocked or failed tracking requests are excluded. Counts are independent of Google Analytics.
