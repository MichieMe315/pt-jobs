PTJC Career Resources

Before accepting a physiotherapy job offer

Get the pay, workload, support and contract terms in writing before you decide.

01. Pay and payment dates

Confirm base salary, hourly rate or fee split, and when payment arrives. For a split, ask which fees, discounts and refunds affect your share.

Ask how assessments, follow-ups, cancellations, reports and insurer-funded visits are paid. Check whether orientation, charting and meetings are paid.

02. Caseload and clinical support

Ask about assessment and follow-up lengths, expected visits per day, overlapping bookings and protected documentation time.

Discuss patient complexity, referral sources, ramp-up expectations, access to mentorship and coverage when you need clinical help.

03. Schedule and paid time

Confirm weekly hours, clinic locations, travel time, evenings, weekends and control over your schedule.

Ask what happens when bookings are light, appointments cancel or a home visit requires extra travel. Get any guaranteed hours in writing.

04. Expenses and equipment

Confirm who pays registration, liability insurance, professional development, software, equipment and treatment supplies.

For contractor work, price accounting, unpaid administration and time away. Ask who handles billing, denied claims and outstanding accounts.

05. Benefits and time off

Confirm vacation pay or paid leave, statutory holidays, sick leave, benefit eligibility and retirement contributions.

Ask when benefits begin, what you contribute and how leave affects a fee split. Check mentorship and education funding separately.

06. Agreement and exit terms

Read the complete agreement and fee schedules before accepting. Check notice periods, repayment clauses and restrictions on future work.

Clarify patient-record responsibilities, continuity of care, outstanding payments and what happens to your caseload when you leave. Get advice on terms you do not understand.
