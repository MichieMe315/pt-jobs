Questions to ask before accepting a physiotherapy job offer

For hospitals, rehabilitation, community care, home care and private practice. Salary and fee splits tell only part of the story. Use this checklist before signing an employee or independent contractor agreement.

1. How is compensation calculated?

  • What is the actual price charged for a typical visit?
  • Is the split calculated before or after discounts, refunds or taxes?
  • Are assessments and follow-up visits paid at different rates?
  • When are completed visits paid, and what happens to late payments?

2. What volume is realistic?

  • What patient volume, acuity and appointment lengths are expected?
  • How is caseload assigned, and what orientation or ramp-up is provided?
  • Who handles referrals, scheduling and follow-up?
  • What is the policy for no-shows and late cancellations?

3. What time is unpaid?

  • Are charting, reports and meetings compensated?
  • Are evenings, weekends or split shifts expected?
  • Who handles billing, insurance forms and administration?
  • Is there guaranteed paid time if bookings are light?

4. Who pays for professional costs?

  • Provincial registration and professional liability insurance?
  • Continuing education, mentorship and courses?
  • Software, equipment, supplies and professional memberships?
  • Accounting, taxes and any required business registration?

5. What benefits and time off apply?

  • Paid vacation, statutory holidays and sick leave?
  • Health/dental coverage and retirement contributions?
  • Paid mentorship, orientation and professional development?
  • What happens to income during vacation or illness?

6. What does the contract restrict?

  • Notice periods and termination conditions?
  • Non-solicitation or restrictive covenants?
  • Who owns patient records and handles continuity of care?
  • Who decides your schedule, fees and clinical processes?

Important: A contract labelled “independent contractor” does not by itself determine employment status for tax or employment law. Seek independent legal/accounting advice where appropriate. Ask for the offer and fee schedule in writing.
